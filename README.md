## About

I'm a dad building Arc Labs on nights and weekends. The studio makes websites, web apps, browser games, and process automation, and it uses its own products to test how AI fits into real work. Discovery AI Index tracks what AI has actually helped discover, Arc Labs Studio Team packages a reusable AI team for coding agents, and Arc School is a homeschool platform for working families. These are the projects I'm working on now.

## Featured Projects

### [Discovery AI Index](https://github.com/arclabshq/discovery-ai-index)

**An evidence-first, human-reviewed catalog of discoveries materially enabled by AI.**

[Discovery AI Index](https://www.discoveryindex.arclabshq.com) explains what changed, why the result matters, how AI contributed, and the strongest evidence available. A daily scanner searches arXiv, bioRxiv, medRxiv, PubMed, and Crossref and adds only private candidates. Records reach the public registry through a separate, authenticated editorial review. The scanner can't publish, edit the repo, or change the schema or thresholds.

`React` · `Vite` · `Cloudflare Workers` · `D1` · `Vercel` · `GitHub Actions`

### [Arc Labs Studio Team](https://github.com/arclabshq/arc-labs-studio-team)

**A portable studio team of reusable AI roles and workflows for Claude, Codex, Gemini, and Grok.**

The bundle uses the open Agent Skills format: one canonical `skills/` directory, 15 focused skills, and thin manifests for each supported client. It includes eight roles (chief of staff, product manager, tech lead, design lead, and more), a first-principles thinking mode, and six workflows such as `github-ship`, `swarm`, and `transfer`. Run `setup-team` to name each role without changing its stable skill ID.

`Agent Skills` · `Claude Code` · `Codex` · `Gemini CLI` · `Grok Build`
